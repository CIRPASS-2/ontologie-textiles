EU DPP Textile Assessment Ontology: Description back to ToC

Assessment methods describe reusable procedures or standards, while assessment executions represent concrete applications of those procedures. Assessment results record the outcome of an execution and remain distinct from the characteristics, phenomena or product aspects being assessed.

The separation between methods, executions and results enables the same method to be reused across products and contexts while preserving the provenance and conditions of individual assessment activities.

The unpublished 2.1.0-dev extension adds resultForAssessedAspect to bind an individual result to its individual product property, degradation phenomenon, failure mode or external damage. The subject is distinct from the measured quantity or grading dimension and from protocol controls. No global cardinality is imposed. Explicit-target SHACL validation is optional and has no cardinality constraint.
