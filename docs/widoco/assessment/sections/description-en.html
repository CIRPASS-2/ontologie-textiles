EU DPP Textile Assessment Ontology: Description back to ToC

Assessment methods describe reusable procedures or standards, while assessment executions represent concrete applications of those procedures. Assessment results record the outcome of an execution and remain distinct from the characteristics, phenomena or product aspects being assessed.

The separation between methods, executions and results enables the same method to be reused across products and contexts while preserving the provenance and conditions of individual assessment activities.

The unpublished 2.1.0-dev extension adds resultForAssessedAspect to bind an individual result to its individual product property, degradation phenomenon, failure mode or external damage. The subject is distinct from the measured quantity or grading dimension and from protocol controls. No global cardinality is imposed. Explicit-target SHACL validation is optional and has no cardinality constraint.

The optional isRepresentedByProductEvent relation links an assessment execution to its conceptual, product-facing ProductEvent, distinct from an EPCIS technical carrier. The existing PROCESS bridge is independent. ASSESSMENT imports the bounded EVENT MIREOT; carrier mapping axioms remain outside that subset. Neither the relation nor OBSERVE / inspecting establishes that every assessment or observation qualifies as a ProductEvent. See TXT-DEC-012 and example 13.

The hasParameterConcept relation supplies categorical protocol values on a MeasurementParameterSetting. Inclusive SHACL alternatives allow numerical, categorical or both forms; numerical checks remain unconditional and concepts have no maximum count. RDFS range inference is not vocabulary-membership validation. Settings, assessed product characteristics and threshold-selection rules remain separate roles. See TXT-DEC-013 and example 14.

The optional awardedScoreValue relation records points actually awarded to an individual assessment result, distinct from its measured value, prescribed rule points and a derived indicator. Supplied values must be scalar decimal QuantitativeValues with units:one. Multiple awards and unscored results are permitted; no calculation, non-negativity or score maximum is added. See TXT-DEC-014 and example 15.
