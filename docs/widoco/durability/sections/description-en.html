EU DPP Textile Durability Ontology: Description back to ToC

Durability constructs, observed performance parameter types and failure modes are represented as OWL classes. They are not replaced by SKOS controlled values in the ontology itself, although application-specific codelists may be derived where controlled data-entry values are required.

Assessment methods, measurements, thresholds and quantitative results remain outside this module and are represented through Textile Assessment and the shared quantitative-value pattern.

Observed degradation phenomena are not declared disjoint from observed performance parameters because the same phenomenon may also function as an inspection parameter in a particular assessment context.

Textile durability specializes CORE durability in the context of the material, construction and appearance characteristics relevant to the product's intended use. This is a project refinement informed by JRC Sections 9.2.1.1, 9.2.1.3 and 13.10.1.1, rather than a verbatim JRC or regulatory definition. Appearance means retention of observable product characteristics, not aesthetic preference or emotional attachment. The detailed JRC evidence concerns apparel; the broader textile interpretation is a project generalization. IntrinsicDurability represents its physical and measurable dimension. Characteristics and test results provide evidence for interpretation, but an individual test result or robustness score does not automatically establish overall durability or service lifetime. The inherited CORE quality-characteristic hierarchy does not make a durability instance a numerical result.
