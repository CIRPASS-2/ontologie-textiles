1 Introduction

Codes are an essential component of any Machine-To-Machine information flow. They have been developed over time to facilitate the flow of standardized data that can be easily validated for correctness to ensure consistent semantics (e.g., plain text versus coded text).

1.1 Objective

The objective of this document is to identify and describe recommended code lists supporting the business processes, and eventually the business transactions, for traceability and transparency in textile and leather value chains.

1.2 Reference documents

Knowledge and application of the following documents is crucial for the development of the information entities specified in this document.

· UNECE Recommendation n°46 on "Enhancing traceability and transparency for sustainable value chain in the garment and footwear sector" (2021)

· UN/CEFACT, Business Requirement Specification for Traceability and Transparency in the Textile and Leather Sector, Part 1: High-Level Process and Data Model (2021).UNECE, Business Process Analysis for Sustainability and Circularity in Textile Value Chains (2021).

· UNECE, Business Process Analysis for Sustainability and Circularity in Leather Value Chains (2021).

· UNECE, Accelerating action for a sustainable and circular garment and footwear industry: which role for transparency and traceability of value chains? Policy paper (2020).

· UNECE, TEXTILE4SDG12: Transparency in Textile Value Chains in Relation to the Environmental, Social and Human Health Impacts of Parts, Components and Production Processes (ECE/TRADE/439).

· UNECE, Traceability for Sustainable Trade: A Framework to Design Traceability Systems for Cross Border Trade (ECE/Trade/429).

· UN/CEFACT Code Management User Guide, 8 March 2018.

1.3 Audience

The audience of this document is all users who are interested in the textile and leather data model, and particularly in the application of identifiers and code lists, supporting information exchange in textile and leather value chains.

1.4 Status of this document

This document will be used as a reference during the implementation phase of messages as described within the supporting UN/CEFACT Business Requirements Specification (BRS) documents for traceability and transparency in the textile and leather sector. The number of code lists and identifiers will change over time. This document may become part of a new project for making it an official UNECE Recommendation.

1.5 Document context

This document is created to support the UNECE - UN/CEFACT project "Sustainable Textile and Leather Traceability and Transparency Project" regarding the Business Information Entities (BIE) used to exchange traceability and transparency as specified within the Business Requirements Specification (BRS) in the Textile and Leather Sector. To support the meaning of code values, this document contains an annex with references to different glossaries for textile and leather, including those developed for the UNECE Recommendation and Business Process Analysis above mentioned

3 Overview of indentifiers

Unique identifiers are important for the traceability of assets and the logistics unit(s) which contain them. Traceable assets and their logistics units make use of unique identifiers. The ISO/IEC 15459 (part 3) specifies a unique, non-significant, identifier (UID) for products and/or items, represented in a barcode label or other media attached to or incorporated into an item. The UID will start with a string of characters representing the issuing agency code, assigned to the issuing agency by a registration authority. There are different agencies which issue UIDs. One of them is GS1.
